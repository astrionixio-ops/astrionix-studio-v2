Our services

A broader continuum of mental health care.

Thrive’s service model brings assessment, treatment, medication support, rehabilitation, crisis intervention, recovery skills, and community-based care together in one connected experience.

01

Mental Health Assessment

Integrated Assessment & Treatment Planning (IATP).

02

Therapy & Counseling

Individual, family, group, and grief/bereavement counseling.

03

Psychotropic Medication Management

Medication administration, monitoring, and training.

04

Mental Health Case Management & Community Support

Coordinated support connecting behavioral health needs with practical community resources.

05

Crisis Intervention

Responsive support focused on immediate behavioral health needs, stabilization, and appropriate next steps.

06

Psychosocial Rehabilitation Service — PSR

Adult Day Program supporting recovery, social functioning, life skills, independence, and community participation.

07

Behavior Intervention, Anger Management & Domestic Violence

Structured behavioral support designed to strengthen emotional regulation, coping skills, accountability, and healthier relationships.

08

Substance Use Prevention & Recovery Life Skills

Practical prevention and recovery-focused skills supporting healthier routines, resilience, and long-term stability.

09

Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT)

Medication-supported treatment services integrated with recovery-oriented care and ongoing support.

“Care should connect clinical treatment with the realities of everyday life.”
Whole-person care

Because mental health doesn’t exist in isolation.

Housing instability, family conflict, financial hardship, trauma, medical concerns, social isolation, unemployment, substance use challenges, and difficulty accessing community resources can all affect mental and emotional health.

Our approach connects treatment with practical, community-based support so people can build skills, resources, relationships, stability, and independence.
