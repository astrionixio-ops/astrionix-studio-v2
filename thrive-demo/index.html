Our services

Support designed around the whole person.

Thrive provides and is developing a continuum of behavioral health services designed to meet individuals where they are and help them move toward greater emotional wellness and stability.

◎

Mental Health Assessment

Understanding symptoms, strengths, needs, functioning, history, supports, and treatment priorities.

◌

Therapy

Individual, family, and group support for emotional, behavioral, interpersonal, and mental health concerns.

♢

Children & Families

Age-appropriate, family-centered care supporting young people through emotional and behavioral challenges.

↗

Psychosocial Rehabilitation

Building practical, social, emotional, and daily-living skills for recovery, functioning, and independence.

＋

Psychiatry & Medication

Developing psychiatric services designed to complement therapy and other behavioral health supports.

⌁

Care Coordination

Helping bridge gaps between behavioral healthcare, medical providers, community resources, and everyday needs.

“Care should connect clinical treatment with the realities of everyday life.”
Whole-person care

Because mental health doesn’t exist in isolation.

Housing instability, family conflict, financial hardship, trauma, medical concerns, social isolation, unemployment, and difficulty accessing community resources can all affect a person’s mental and emotional health.

Our approach extends beyond the therapy room, connecting treatment with practical community-based support so people can build skills, resources, relationships, stability, and independence.
